Privacy Policy

Nova Lumora Inc · Nashville, Tennessee · Effective September 17, 2026

Nova Lumora Inc ("Nova Lumora," "we," "us") provides AI-powered marketing and automation services for businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you contact us, request a free marketing plan, or fill out a form (on our website or through an ad, such as a Facebook or Instagram lead form), we collect the information you provide: typically your name, business name, email address, and phone number. If you call our office line, the call is answered by an AI assistant and the conversation may be transcribed and summarized so we can follow up on your request.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, prepare the materials you requested (such as a free marketing plan), provide and improve our services, and contact you about your request by phone, text message, or email. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers to operate our business — for example hosting, communications (such as Twilio), scheduling, analytics, and AI processing. These providers handle your information only to perform services for us.

Data from Meta lead ads

If you submit your information through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, we receive only what you chose to submit, and we use it solely to deliver what you requested and follow up about our services, consistent with Meta's Lead Ads Terms.

Retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, and we use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect it.

Your choices

You can opt out of communications at any time — reply STOP to any text, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or contact us directly. You may also request access to or deletion of your information by emailing james@novalumorainc.com.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here with a new effective date.
